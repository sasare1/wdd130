Takoradi, Ghana

Sekondi-Takoradi is a city in the western region of Ghana, sometimes called the 'Twin City'. Sekondi and Takoradi are the two adjoining cities that bred the city's name; Sekondi is the political administration centre and Takoradi is like the business hub. Second to Cape Three Points, Sekondi-Takoradi's land area extends Ghana's border with the Atlantic Ocean further south into the sea. The native language is Fante, one of the Akan dialect. The city hosts a harbor and a number of tourist destinations. Although there is 'second' in the city's name, that is not how 'Secondi' came by.
